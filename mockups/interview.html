§03 What access looks like

Gated states
Pending

Request sent. It lands in my inbox and waits for a human — no auto-approve, no link until I say so.

Approved

Approved — link emailed. A single-use, time-boxed session opens the interview with a per-session token budget.

Expired

Session expired. The budget or time window closed. Request a fresh link any time — no penalty.
